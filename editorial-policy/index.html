Editorial policy

Our instructions should be testable, precise, original, and useful at the board.

In this guide · Play · Rules · About · Contact

Editorial scope

We publish explanations of Numble mechanics, equation construction, feedback, Daily play, scoring, practice, and solving strategy. The site also documents its generator and privacy choices. Material stays focused on this game and ordinary arithmetic needed to play it; we do not present puzzle guidance as professional education, financial advice, or a substitute for a formal curriculum.

Originality and usefulness

Game copy and guides are written for this site. Established conventions such as operator precedence are explained in our own language and connected to actual board decisions. We prefer a direct answer near the beginning, short sections, checklists, tables, and examples that can be verified. Content should help a reader make a better next move rather than pad a page with generic mathematics.

Fact checking

Equation examples are recalculated for equality and reviewed for the exact character length, legal operators, leading-zero rule, non-negative result, standard precedence, and exact division. Claims about feedback, local statistics, date seeding, and validation are checked against the implementation. Strategy statements are framed as practical methods rather than guarantees when several candidate equations can fit the same clues.

Updates and corrections

We review reports that identify a page, claim, and reason for correction. Confirmed arithmetic, rule, or technical errors are corrected promptly where practical. A substantive revision changes the visible update date; minor typography fixes may not. Send a concise report through contact, including a calculation or reproduction steps when relevant.

AI-assisted work

Automated tools may assist with drafting, consistency checks, or locating passages that need review. They do not replace arithmetic verification or comparison with the live implementation. We do not knowingly publish fabricated percentages, player ratings, testimonials, research findings, or unsupported claims of optimal play. Final responsibility for what appears on the site remains with the publisher.

Commercial independence

Advertising does not determine puzzle answers, equation selection, rule explanations, strategies, or editorial conclusions. We do not rank tactics because an advertiser requests it. Analytics may show which pages are visited, but popularity alone does not turn an unverified statement into a fact.

Reader standard

Readers should be able to test a claim using the rules, the working game, or the transparent generator explanation. The basics guide demonstrates the level of concrete explanation we aim for throughout the site.
