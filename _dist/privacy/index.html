Privacy policy

The puzzle does not require an account. Game progress and statistics stay on your device.

In this guide · Play · About · Terms · Contact

Game data stored locally

Numble uses browser localStorage for completed-game counts, wins, current and best Daily streaks, and separate Classic and Mini top times. This information supports the statistics shown back to you on the same browser. The game does not upload those records to a Numble account or game database. Clearing this site’s stored data, using private browsing, or changing browsers can reset or separate those records.

Analytics

Google Analytics is loaded only after you grant "analytics" consent in the privacy-choices dialog; it helps measure visits and understand general use of the site. If you decline, the site works without it. You can reopen the dialog via the "Privacy choices" button at the bottom-left of every page.

Advertising

Google AdSense is loaded only after you grant "advertising" consent; before that no ad scripts run and no ad cookies are written. You may withdraw consent any time via the "Privacy choices" button (stored locally, key gh_consent_v1).

Hosting and security logs

The hosting and network providers that deliver the site may process standard request information, including IP address, requested path, time, user agent, and security events. Such operational logs can be used to serve pages, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and protect the service. They are not the same as the game statistics held in localStorage.

Messages you send

If you email us, the message, address, and any information you voluntarily include are used to understand and answer the request, investigate a report, and maintain necessary correspondence. Do not include sensitive personal information. See contact guidance for the details useful in a bug or correction report.

Your controls

You can clear site data in browser settings to remove local Numble records, block or delete cookies, use available consent controls, or stop using the site. Browser privacy tools can also affect whether stats persist between visits. Because no game account exists, we cannot restore a local streak after deletion or move it to another device.
