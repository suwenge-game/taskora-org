Arithmetic, distilled

Numble Daily is an independent one-game site built around logical equation guessing.

In this guide · Play · Rules · Daily · Contact

What the site is for

Numble turns a small piece of mental arithmetic into a repeatable deduction puzzle. The aim is not to reward obscure formulas or fast calculator use. It is to help players notice equation structure, test evidence, and build a true expression under a strict character limit. Classic mode offers the full eight-character puzzle; Mini compresses the same ideas into six characters.

How the game is made

The game interface, candidate generator, arithmetic evaluator, duplicate-aware feedback, and guides are original to this site. There is no embedded third-party game. Candidate equations are screened for exact length, legal syntax, whole-number division, standard precedence, and equal values on both sides. The generator note describes that process without revealing today’s answer.

How we produce content

Guides begin with behavior that can be checked against the working game. Equation examples are calculated and reviewed under the same rules as submitted rows. We favor direct explanations, short practice steps, and examples that players can reproduce. Substantive corrections receive a new visible update date under the standards in our editorial policy.

Privacy by design

No account is required. Completed games, streaks, and saved times remain in local browser storage and can be removed by clearing this site’s data. The game does not upload those records. Analytics and advertising are separate third-party services described in the privacy policy.

What comes next

Future improvements will stay focused on clearer learning material, accessible play, reliable validation, and useful practice paths. The basics guide, Mini guide, and frequency guide expand the learning path while keeping the game itself compact. Suggestions and reproducible bug reports are welcome through contact.
