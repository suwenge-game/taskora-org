A submitted game counts as completed when it is won or all six valid rows are used. Invalid equations are rejected before consuming a row, so they do not increase the wrong-guess count. Practice helps technique, while the calendar streak belongs specifically to Daily wins.

How leaderboard order works

Classic and Mini records are kept separately because their equation lengths create different solving conditions. Within each local top-ten list, results are ordered first by fewer wrong guesses and then by shorter elapsed time. A clean solve therefore ranks ahead of a faster solve that required more incorrect valid rows. If wrong-guess totals match, time breaks the tie.

Daily streak behavior

A streak grows when you win the Daily puzzle on consecutive local dates. Missing a date or losing breaks the current run, while Best preserves the longest previous run. Refreshing does not change today’s answer. The local calendar controls the day boundary, so changing device time can make a displayed date differ from what you expect.

Storage and privacy

Stats live only in this browser’s localStorage. They are not uploaded to a Numble account.

Clearing site data, using a private window, moving to another browser, or changing devices can remove or separate records. There is no account-based restore or cross-device sync. This design keeps puzzle history local but makes your browser responsible for persistence.

Common questions

Does an invalid row hurt my score? No; it must be a valid equation before it becomes a guess. Are Mini and Classic times mixed? No; they have separate lists. Can support restore a streak? No, because the saved value stays on your device.

Practice sequencing for this material

Skills from this page land in this order: first run the drill on a worksheet set (timed, untidy is fine), then apply it to the daily equation the same day, then teach it back — a written sentence in your notes explaining the rule to a hypothetical beginner. The teaching-back step is the retention mechanism; skips there decay within a week.

Expect a three-day consolidation window per skill. Day one feels mechanical, day two feels ambiguous, day three it starts firing unprompted. Players who quit on day two never reach the payoff, which is why this site spaces its worksheet sets rather than batching them — spacing is the method, not a compromise.

Fair-difficulty guarantee

The daily equation is generated under constraints this site documents openly: integer division results, standard precedence, and a deterministic daily seed. No guesswork spikes, no hidden difficulty days. If a board feels unfair, check two things before suspecting the puzzle: every division in your candidate must be integer-clean at each step, and every mixed-operator reading must follow standard precedence. Those two checks resolve the overwhelming majority of "impossible" boards.

The same constraints make the site's advice durable: because the generator's rules are fixed and published, techniques derived from them cannot be patched out from under you. What changes is the calendar, not the mathematics.
