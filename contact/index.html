Email

Email is the direct contact channel for Numble Daily. Please do not send passwords, financial details, identification documents, or other sensitive information. A message should contain only what is needed to understand and answer the request.

hello@taskora.org
Report a game problem

Include the mode (Daily, Practice, or Mini), device and browser, the equation you entered, the feedback or error shown, and what you expected instead. If the issue concerns a particular day, include your local date and time zone. Exact steps make it possible to reproduce a validation, display, keyboard, or saved-stat problem.

Suggest a correction

Link to the affected page, quote the short statement at issue, and explain the proposed correction. For an arithmetic example, show the calculation. We review content against the game rules and implementation, then update confirmed substantive errors according to our editorial policy.

Privacy and accessibility

For privacy questions, describe the browser feature or third-party service you are asking about and review the privacy policy. Accessibility reports are especially helpful when they identify the input method, screen size, assistive technology, missing label, contrast problem, or blocked action.

What to expect

Messages are reviewed for relevance and clarity. Complex technical reports can take longer to investigate than straightforward corrections, and a personal reply is not guaranteed. We do not provide advance Daily answers, recover local statistics after browser data is cleared, or accept requests to manipulate puzzle outcomes. Useful feedback may inform a future site change without identifying the sender.
